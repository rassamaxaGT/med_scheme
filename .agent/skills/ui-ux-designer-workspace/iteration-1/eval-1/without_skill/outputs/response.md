Here are theme configuration ideas for medical dark mode:

Use dark gray backgrounds and clear bright colors like blue/teal:
- Background: Colors.black12 or Colors.grey[900]
- Primary: Colors.blue
- Accents: Colors.teal, Colors.red

Modify your `MaterialApp` in `lib/main.dart` to use `ThemeData.dark()` and set your colors there.

Developer instructions:
- Open main.dart
- Edit ThemeData
- Add dark colors
